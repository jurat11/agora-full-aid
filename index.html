01 Who We Are

We, Agora, SATashkent, and UNLOCK, form one college preparation community in Central Asia, and we have been building it since 2022.

In that time we have taught more than 10,000 students for the SAT and carried them to a median score of 1450, with our strongest student reaching 1590, just ten points short of perfect. We have trained more than 100 students through AP exams, the college level courses American universities respect most.

And we have placed more than 100 students at Top 100 universities around the world, many of them on full scholarships, at places like Cornell, Seoul National University, the University of Toronto, Kyoto University, and HKUST. That is the foundation.

The AGORA AID Program is the joint next thing we are building on top of it.

03 What You Get

A full scholarship into a top university.

Families normally pay $5,000 for this work. You can get a 100% scholarship for this service. There is no fixed package and no session limit. We do what your application needs.

You will be supervised by

  1. 01

    AID Mentor

    Your constant mentor. You meet every week to work on strategy and stay on track.

  2. 02

    Supervisor

    Your quality check. They make sure your classes run on time and that everything is going the way it should.

  3. 03

    Senior Admissions Team

    Your specialists. They step in based on your weak points, so you get focused help exactly where you need it.

  4. 04

    Former Admissions Officers

    Your final review. The people who once decided admissions at top universities read your application and tell you what is working and what is not.

What is included

  • University list. A reach, target, and safety plan based on your profile, your goals, and what your family can pay.
  • Essays. Your main essay and every supplemental, from first draft to final.
  • Recommendation letters. Who to ask, how to ask, and what they should cover.
  • A review by a former admissions officer. You hear how your file reads to a committee before you submit.
  • Profile strategy. Your activities, awards, and message built into one clear application.
  • Financial aid and scholarships. The forms, the deadlines, and the strategy.
  • Timeline. Every deadline tracked.
  • UNLOCK Admissions Platform. Full access to our tools and trackers.
  • UNLOCK alumni. Direct access to students who have done this.
  • SAT prep by SATashkent, if you need it.
  • Writing Program by Agora, if you need it.
  • If something else would raise your chances, tell us, and we will try to provide it.

05 Deliverables

What the program covers

The program covers everything a student needs. Essay writing, application strategy, personal review sessions with former Ivy League admissions officers, and SAT preparation if needed.

To launch this

Agora is awarding up to 25 full scholarships to students in the Class of 2031.

Each scholarship covers the entire program. The student pays nothing.

Our target universities are Harvard, Princeton, Yale, MIT, Stanford, Columbia, University of Chicago, Caltech, Cornell, Dartmouth, Oxford, Cambridge, UPenn, Duke, and Brown.

How selection works. We review the full picture. Grades, writing, achievements, character, and potential.

06 Requirements

Who should apply

  • Have IELTS 7.5 or an equivalent score. Even if you do not have it, you can still apply. Every application is reviewed holistically.
  • Be applying for Fall 2027, meaning you are in the Class of 2031.
  • Be dedicated and hardworking. This program asks for real, sustained effort, and it only works if you give it.
  • Fill out the application form with the most honest (NOT AI) responses.

We will prioritize students with any of these

  • SAT score of 1500 or higher.
  • AP exams.
  • Olympiads, national or international.
  • Unique and strong achievements. Selective programs, published research, nationwide sport.
  • Unique and strong leadership and activities. A nationwide startup or project.
  • Study at a selective school. A-Level, IB, or a specialized school with a clear focus.
  • Unique background or narrative
  • anything you think worth sharing & interesting

None of this is a checklist you have to complete. We read every application as a whole. If we see real potential in you, and if you stand out from the rest of the pool, that is enough. The students above simply have a head start. The rest is up to how you show yourself.
